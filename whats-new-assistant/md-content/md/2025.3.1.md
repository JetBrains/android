## Studio Labs features available

Studio Labs lets you try out the latest AI experimental features in a stable
version of Android Studio, so you can more quickly integrate our AI assistance
offerings in your development workflow. To enable the features, go to
**Studio Labs** in settings.

[Learn more ↗](https://d.android.com/r/studio-ui/gemini/labs)

## Simplified JDK management with Gradle Daemon JVM Criteria

To simplify JDK management for Gradle builds, Android Studio now uses
[Gradle Daemon JVM criteria](https://docs.gradle.org/current/userguide/gradle_daemon.html#sec:configuring_daemon_jvm)
by default for new projects. This feature lets Gradle auto-detect a compatible
JDK for your project on your machine or auto-provision the required JDK by
downloading it if it can't be found locally.

This simplifies project setup, reduces setup-related errors, and ensures that
JDK selection for Gradle builds is consistent across different machines and
between the IDE and command line.

[Learn more ↗](https://d.android.com/r/studio-ui/build/daemon-jvm-criteria)
